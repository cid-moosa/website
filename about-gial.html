🏛️ Institutional Overview

Girideepam Institute of Advanced Learning (GIAL) is the higher education segment of the Girideepam Group of Institutions, which has an esteemed educational heritage dating back to 1975 in Kottayam, Kerala. Established to provide exemplary collegiate instruction, GIAL is intensively involved in its efforts to elevate society through community-oriented undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

The college is renowned for combining academic rigor with strong moral conviction. The School of Management Studies is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and all university degree programs are affiliated to Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam.

⛪ Bethany Navajyothi Province (Bethany Ashram)

Girideepam Institute of Advanced Learning is owned and administered by the Bethany Navajyothi Province of the Order of the Imitation of Christ (OIC - Bethany Ashram). The congregation was founded on August 15, 1919, by the Servant of God, Archbishop Mar Ivanios, a visionary educationist and pioneer of spiritual renewal in the Malankara Catholic Church.

The Bethany Fathers have dedicated decades to nation-building through high-quality education, healthcare, and social upliftment across India. GIAL stands as a pinnacle milestone in this sacred journey of imparting wisdom that is deeply grounded in human values and ethical principles.

The RICRAC Character Model

GIAL instills the signature RICRAC framework to ensure every student graduates as an ethical, accountable, and capable leader.

R
Responsibility
Taking ownership of one's academic pursuits, ethical duties, and social commitment to the broader community.
I
Integrity
Unwavering commitment to honesty, intellectual transparency, and ethical conduct in all professional endeavors.
C

Empathy, compassionate mentorship, and active service towards fellow students and underprivileged sections of society.

📜 Statutory Approvals & Accreditation

All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE): The School of Management Studies at GIAL operates with official annual AICTE Extension of Approval (EOA), fulfilling all statutory norms for technical and management education.

Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam: All degree programs (BBA, BCA, B.Sc Cyber Forensics, B.Com, B.Sc Psychology, M.Com, MSW) adhere to the university-approved Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) curriculum and examination regulations.

ISO 9001:2015 Certification: Quality management systems for curriculum execution, lab operations, and student support services comply with international ISO 9001 standards.
